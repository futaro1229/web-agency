Why Stay with Us?

A fully renovated ryotei ryokan blending 75 years of tradition with modern comfort — welcoming families, international travelers, and all guests.

  • Exquisite Dining: Authentic Kobe beef kaiseki at Restaurant Kochou (胡蝶), using DNA-verified Kobe beef and Seto Inland Sea ingredients.
  • Private Onsen: Three reservable baths fed by Rokko Arima Onsen — famed as the “beauty bath” for its soft, moisturizing waters.
  • Prime Access: 5 min walk from Shin-Kobe Shinkansen Station · 20 min drive from Kobe Airport · free airport shuttle for international flights.
  • Inclusive: Proudly LGBTQ-friendly and welcoming to all guests. Semi-universal design throughout the property.

Dining at Kochou

Seasonal kaiseki celebrating Kobe’s land and sea — from breakfast set menus to premium Kobe beef courses.

Kobe Beef Kaiseki

At Restaurant Kochou (料亭 胡蝶), DNA-verified Kobe beef takes center stage — thinly sliced for shabu-shabu in golden dashi, alongside sashimi, seasonal appetizers, and Seto Inland Sea delicacies.

  • Multi-course kaiseki from ¥8,800 (lunch) to premium beef courses
  • 神戸牛づくし会席 — steak, shabu-shabu, and nigiri in one meal
  • Day visits welcome — lunch kaiseki without an overnight stay
